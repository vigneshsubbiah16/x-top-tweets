How it works

  • 1. Enter any Twitter/X username
  • 2. Select the time period (1 or 3 months)
  • 3. Get the top 10 tweets ranked by engagement

Engagement Formula

Score = Likes + (Retweets × 2) + Replies + Quotes + Bookmarks

Ranking combines absolute engagement (70%) and engagement rate relative to followers (30%).
